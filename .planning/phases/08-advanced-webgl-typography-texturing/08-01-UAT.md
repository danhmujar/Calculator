# Phase 08-01 UAT: Dynamic Texture Atlas & SDF Generation

## Test 1: Dynamic Atlas Stress Test
**Description**: Verify that entering a large number of unique mathematical symbols correctly triggers the atlas to pack and generate SDFs.
**Steps**:
1. Open the calculator.
2. Enter a series of 20+ unique Greek letters and math operators (e.g., `\alpha\beta\gamma\delta\epsilon\zeta\eta\theta\iota\kappa\lambda\mu\nu\xi\omicron\pi\rho\sigma\tau\upsilon\phi\chi\psi\omega\forall\exists\in\notin\ni\prod\sum\int`).
3. Observe the console for any "Atlas Full" errors or glitches.
4. Verify via WebGL debugger (if possible) or by visual inspection that glyphs are sharp and correctly rendered.
**Expected**: All glyphs are sharp, no errors, and the atlas handles the load efficiently.
**Status**: [PASSED]
**Evidence**: Verified via `tests/visual-debug.spec.js` which generated a successful visual capture of the atlas at `test-results/visual-verification/texture-atlas-08-01.png`. Packing and SDF generation confirmed for 30+ symbols.
